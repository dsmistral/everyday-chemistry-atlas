### N26 upgrade — NurdRage, "Efficiently Recover Nitric Acid and Copper Metal From Copper Nitrate Wastes" → transcript level
- Verified sources: https://filmot.com/video/l8uQWibpRYk (caption transcript; date corroborated 2020-11-22; 37.3K views at fetch).
- Upgrade content — **the copper-recovery step RESOLVED: electrolysis**. Overall: Cu(NO3)2 + H2SO4 → HNO3 distilled off; the CuSO4 solution is then electrolyzed (large copper cathode at the beaker bottom — "lets us do the electrodeposition without stirring"; platinum electrode) so copper plates out and the H2SO4 regenerates in-cell ("you just need a platinum electrode and some sulfuric acid but both are reused"). The copper-oxide-waste variant is given explicitly: titrate the CuO residue into 10% H2SO4, boil to drive dissolution, then electrodeposition as in the main route. Caveat: "you shouldn't have any precious metals like silver in solution alongside the copper" (they will co-deposit). Framed against N21: "much better than having to deal with toxic nitrogen dioxide gas as in the thermal decomposition method."
- Decisive moves upgraded: **P5 (new firing at upgrade level — the electrolytic copper recovery that closes the Cu cycle with N09/N15)**, P10 (A/B vs N21 sharpened to hazard economics), P1, P4, P2.

### N28 upgrade — NurdRage, "Make SULFURIC acid from WEAK oxalic acid with the SUN" → transcript level
- Verified sources: **URL pinned https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iDlFEV8bT3c** (date corroborated 2024-06-22; 27.6K views at fetch); transcript at https://filmot.com/video/iDlFEV8bT3c.
- Upgrade content — **"with the SUN" RESOLVED: iron(III) oxalate is photosensitive.** After the main FeSO4 + oxalic acid run, the beaker was left in direct sunlight for 8 hours before filtering: "iron (iii) oxalate is actually photosensitive and photo decomposes into iron (II) oxalate and carbon dioxide... a well known reaction and used for some types of early photography before the development of more advanced silver halide based processes... it's clean and the resulting carbon dioxide easily boils out." Then filtered, boiled down, distilled.
- Honest-failure datum: **the magnesium-oxalate variant FAILED — "the yield was under 2%. 2% is completely useless for the amateur."** The magnesium oxalate "just wasn't stable enough to drive the reaction forward. So the sulfuric acid produced simply reacted with the magnesium oxalate and drove it back again." Community misinterpretation: "they weren't lying, they just misinterpreted their results" — the massive magnesium sulfate residue in the boiling flask was misread as product. Route-economics line: "You might as well spend the same money buying copper sulfate and electrolyzing it, the previously most expensive way to make sulfuric acid. At least this magnesium oxalate process now gains that title."
- Decisive moves upgraded: **P10 honest-failure face (new firing at upgrade level — chains N35)**, P14 fail-mode evidence (the "not insoluble/stable enough" failure face recorded at book P14), P1/P3/P4/P2 as gathered; the photochemistry datum links Ch 32 (the early-photography precedent = the book's light-sensitive chemistry chapter).
- Feeds: Ch 15, 24, 32, 36.
