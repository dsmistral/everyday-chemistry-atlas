### N07. Make Hydrazine Sulfate by the Hypochlorite-Ketazine Process — The Complete Guide — NurdRage (date unverified)
- Verified URLs: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UB7vwIFCnR0 ; author mirror: https://www.instructables.com/Make-Hydrazine-Sulfate-by-the-Hypochlorite-Ketazin/ ; WonderHowTo copy. Corroboration: ScienceMadness threads building "the NurdRage method" with scaled recipes.
- Verification level: content-verified (author's Instructables mirror: 250 mL ammonia + 100 mL MEK + ~1/4 mol-equivalent sodium hypochlorite bleach, ~186 g of 10%).
- Faithful summary: hypochlorite oxidizes ammonia toward hydrazine; MEK present in the pot immediately binds nascent hydrazine as KETAZINE (an azine protecting group), preventing over-oxidation; after the reaction the ketazine is hydrolyzed and the solution acidified with sulfuric acid to precipitate hydrazine sulfate crystals. Warnings: toxic gases, corrosive, hydrazine highly toxic/carcinogenic.

### N08. Make Iodine from an Alkali Metal Iodide, Hydrochloric Acid and Hydrogen Peroxide — NurdRage (2009-04-02)
- Verified URLs: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZFP7fYGrFRI ; author mirror: https://www.instructables.com/How-to-make-iodine-from-an-Alkali-metal-iodide-hy/ ; date via Internet Archive NurdRage collection ("20090402 - ... [ZFP7fYGrFRI]"); IMDb episode entry (2009).
- Verification level: content-verified (author mirror carries the full procedure).
- Faithful summary: dissolve KI/NaI in minimal water; add equal volume of concentrated HCl; add five volumes of 3% hydrogen peroxide; let stand ~10 min; elemental iodine precipitates — filter it, purify by vaporization (sublimation) and crystallization. Warning: small amounts of chlorine gas; distilled water preferred (contaminants catalyze Cl2 formation).

### N09. Make Copper Sulfate from Copper and Sulfuric Acid (3 Ways) — NurdRage
- Verified URLs: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=arlYPz3EP7A ; author mirror: https://www.instructables.com/Make-Copper-Sulfate-from-Copper-and-Sulfuric-acid-/ ; WonderHowTo copy.
- Verification level: content-verified (author mirror).
- Faithful summary: copper does not directly react with sulfuric acid, so an oxidant is needed. Two chemical methods (hydrogen peroxide as oxidant; nitric acid as oxidant — emits NO2) and one electrochemical method (DC power supply, copper anode dissolves into H2SO4 electrolyte). Crystallize CuSO4·5H2O. Route economics: electricity is the cheapest per gram of product; battery charge is too small-scale; chemical oxidants cost more.
- Decisive moves: OTC sourcing (copper wire/tubing, drain-cleaner H2SO4, peroxide) [P1]; electrolysis as the oxidant [P5]; crystallization [P2]; 3-way cost comparison [P10 — the exemplar].
- Candidates: none new.
