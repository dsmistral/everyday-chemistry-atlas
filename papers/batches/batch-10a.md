### N52. "Synthesis of Hydrazine Sulfate via The Hoffmann Rearrangement Process on Urea (with Ketone Azine Final Workup)" — Hexavalent, ScienceMadness member publication (S13)

- Verification level reached: full-document read (the entire 4,895-character PDF: abstract, introduction, complete experimental with quantities, experimenter's notes, references)
- Faithful summary: an OTC-only hydrazine sulfate synthesis written by a ScienceMadness forum member. Abstract: all chemicals "purchased easily from local stores or online (from eBay only)"; WARNING block (hydrazine intermediates/salts toxic, potentially carcinogenic, reactive; full PPE; ducted fume cupboard or outside). Introduction: the Hoffmann rearrangement on urea completed at 42% yield as a glistening free-flowing white crystalline powder; urea reacts with hypochlorite in basic aqueous solution to generate an aminoisocyanate intermediate, hydrolysed to hydrazine + Na2CO3; MEK then forms methyl ethyl ketazine (butanone azine), separated and treated with sulfuric acid to precipitate hydrazine sulfate, freeing the excess MEK. Experimental with full quantities: 314 g of 12.5% NaOCl chilled in an ice bath on a stirrer; 53 g granular NaOH added in small portions (exotherm; NaClO degrades at temperature); 39 g recrystallized urea + 600 mg gelatine + 50 mL water in a 1 L RBF (solutes dissolved by low-setting microwave heating — an improvisation datum); claisen adapter + pressure-equalizing dripping funnel + cooled reflux condenser; the basic hypochlorite added at 1 drop/sec, NO heat, continuous stirring; the mixture turns yellow/brown; heated slowly to 90 C at reflux — "when it goes clear the reaction has finished"; cooled below 40 C; 77 g of 99.9% MEK poured through the condenser (condenser left in place to prevent hydrazine vapour leakage); 3 h vigorous stirring forms the azine; separatory-funnel separation (clean — the aqueous layer is nearly saturated with NaCl formed by the reaction); in a separate vessel 40 mL of 18M (~98%) H2SO4 diluted into 100 mL water, then combined with stirring; some hydrazine sulfate crystallizes at once, then azeotropic distillation removes excess MEK and drives the precipitation to completion — the 77.3 C fraction (the MEK/H2O azeotrope) is taken and RECYCLED for another batch; ice-bath cooling; vacuum filtration; air pulled over the solid 30 min to dry on the frit; amber glass storage. Yield: 42% based on hypochlorite = 29.229 g = 0.225 mol N2H4.H2SO4. Experimenter's note: a large amount of foam during the hypochlorite addition, subsiding with powerful stirring and time. References: NurdRage's UB7vwIFCnR0 (= N07); UnintentionalChaos' JCrDttuw5co; the ScienceLab MSDS; Wikipedia.
