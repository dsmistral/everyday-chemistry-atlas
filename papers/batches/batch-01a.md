- Verification level: content-verified (author's own Instructables mirror carries the method; archive.org confirms date).
- Faithful summary: concentrated sulfuric acid from sodium metabisulfite (brewing/pool supply) + hydrochloric acid (muriatic) + oxidant (3% H2O2 or nitric acid).
