- Verification level reached: transcript-level.
- Faithful summary: the from-scratch route — sulfur burned, SO2 absorbed in water containing bromine; the bromine oxidizes SO2 (→ H2SO4) and is itself reduced to HBr; **the bromine is regenerated electrolytically in-cell**: 200 mL of 5 M HBr as electrolyte, carbon electrodes + a copper electrode, 2 A ("this might be too much but this is the first time I'm doing this, gotta start somewhere").
