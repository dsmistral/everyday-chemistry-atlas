### N28. Make SULFURIC acid from WEAK oxalic acid with the SUN — NurdRage (circa 2024-06)
- Verified URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iDlFEV8bT3c (verified 2026-10-15 via YouTube watch page channel attribution "NurdRage" + description "make sulfuric acid by the oxalate precipitation method... oxalic acid produces insoluble metal oxalates" + ScienceMadness thread "Notes on the new NurdRage video: making sulphuric acid from sulphates and oxalic acid" posted 23-6-2024 by Keras https://www.sciencemadness.org/whisper/viewthread.php?tid=160390 + NurdRage Patreon post of 2024-07-13 calling the oxalate-HNO3 video "a follow up on making sulfuric acid by oxalate precipitation")
- Verification level reached: content-verified (video description + same-week independent forum replication; transcript fetch deferred; the "with the SUN" element unverified — flagged for the transcript pass)
- Faithful summary: sulfuric acid from a sulfate salt + oxalic acid: although oxalic acid is far weaker than sulfuric acid, the insolubility of most metal oxalates displaces the equilibrium toward products, leaving H2SO4 in solution behind a filterable oxalate precipitate. The video tests iron(II) sulfate. Community extension (Keras, ScienceMadness, 23-6-2024): replicated with copper(II) sulfate — 6 g CuSO4 in ~20 mL water + 3.8 g oxalic acid dihydrate (slight excess) at room temperature; immediate light-blue copper(II) oxalate precipitate; vacuum filtration over glass fiber + diatomaceous earth; notes the video's own iron(II) oxalate crystallizes as a fine powder that passes a fritted funnel (process-failure datum for the Fe route); computes the route can in theory reach ~8 M (~50% w/w) H2SO4 since reactants are consumed and water is liberated.
- Decisive moves: OTC sulfate + oxalic acid [P1] ; insoluble-byproduct metathesis [P3] ; precipitation pulls the proton transfer [P4] ; filtration as the separation [P2] ; sulfate-source choice (Fe filters badly, Cu is clean) [P10] — CAND-7 FIRED (second batch firing — the H2SO4 face; recurrence test PASSED)
