# CAND-5: Protective-intermediate staging

Shape: a needed intermediate (hydrazine) is too reactive to survive the very conditions that produce it (hypochlorite oxidation of ammonia over-oxidizes it).
Move: keep a masking reagent in the pot (MEK) that instantly binds the nascent species as a stable derivative (ketazine); after the harsh step, hydrolyze to unmask it and precipitate as a storable salt (hydrazine sulfate).
Fired: B02a N07.
Watch: other over-oxidation/over-reduction traps; in-situ protection as a general network move.
Promotion test: fires across >= 2 batches.
