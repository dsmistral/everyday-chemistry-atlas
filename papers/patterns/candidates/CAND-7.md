# CAND-7: Precipitation-driven acid liberation

Shape: want a strong acid (HNO3, H2SO4, ...); have its OTC salt; NO strong-acid precursor and no distillation wanted.
Move: metathesis with an acid whose metal-salt byproduct is INSOLUBLE — the byproduct's precipitation pulls the proton-transfer equilibrium, leaving the free acid in solution; filter off the precipitate.
This is the P3/P4 intersection: P3's insoluble-byproduct trick applied to P4's acid-liberation target — acid-making without distillation and without a stronger-acid precursor.
Fired: B03a N16 (calcium ammonium nitrate + oxalic acid -> Ca oxalate precipitate + HNO3 in solution).
Watch: ScienceMadness thread extending the same method to sulphates ("making sulphuric acid from sulphates and oxalic acid", https://www.sciencemadness.org/whisper/viewthread.php?tid=160390) — a possible H2SO4 face; citric/tartaric-acid analogues; CaCO3-removal cases.
Promotion test: fires across >= 2 batches.
