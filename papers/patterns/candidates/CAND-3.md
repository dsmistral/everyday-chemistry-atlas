# CAND-3: Protective medium + selective dissolution

Shape: product is reactive/molten (sodium) and must be isolated from slag/byproducts without pressure or inert-atmosphere gear.
Move: run the reaction under a compatible liquid barrier (mineral oil), then separate product from slag by a solvent that dissolves one and not the other (dioxane dissolves Na, not MgO).
Fired: B01b N04.
Watch: other reactive-metal or low-melting-product isolations; mercury-free metal handling.
Promotion test: fires across >= 2 batches.
