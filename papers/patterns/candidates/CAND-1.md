# CAND-1: In-situ redox generation (dissolved-gas route)

Shape: need an acid/oxidized product whose natural precursor is a volatile gas (SO2, Cl2, HCl vapor...).
Move: generate the gas IN the pot from OTC reagents (salt + acid) and oxidize/consume it in solution immediately — never handle bulk gas.
Fired: B01a N01 (metabisulfite + HCl -> SO2, oxidized by H2O2 to H2SO4).
Watch: iodine-from-iodide videos (HCl + H2O2 in-situ halogen generation) in batch 2; P5 (electrolytic gas generation) is the apparatus-heavy alternative.
Promotion test: fires across >= 2 batches.
