# Engine 65 record — the rational-tape engine (session 171, the Ch 45 Solvent Shelf authoring)

## 1. Design spec

Engine 65, the rational-tape engine — fresh at session 171, sharing zero code with Engines 1–64. Every value an exact rational `{n, d}` of float64-safe integers under a 9.0e15 magnitude guard; an iterative gcd at every constructor; addition and subtraction by cross-multiplication; multiplication and division pre-divided by both cross-gcds; printing by round-half-up on the scaled integer `floor((2n·p + d)/2d)`. No BigInt, no recursion, no `Math.random`; the float64 lane carries witnesses only, never values. Each row runs three lanes: the exact string, an independent float witness (plain formulas, `.toFixed`), and the tape's own reduced fraction printed at every ledger row — `exactfrac` — so the transcript itself carries the proof of the arithmetic.

Distinctness from the prior series: Engines 1–62 are schoolbook string-integer digit arithmetic (base-10 digit strings, exact digit-string rationals at Engine 62); Engine 63 (the Ch 44 authoring engine) is the series' first binary-limb mini-float with its atanh-series transcendental lane; Engine 64 is the Ch 44 review engine. Engine 65 is the series' rational-tape engine: exact fractions held as integer pairs in the float64-safe range, with the reduced fraction printed as a first-class output lane. The design reason is the chapter's own: the Solvent Shelf's rows are chains — charges → moles → masses → densities → yields — and chains are where rounding order becomes a finding (L05c is the chapter's own lesson: the register's 85.5% is what the arithmetic gives only if you round the intermediate first; the exact chain gives 85.6).
