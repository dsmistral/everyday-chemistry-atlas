## Design

- **The number type.** Every quantity a signed fixed-point decimal on an integer digit array. Charges on the microgram lattice (integers, µg); molar masses in integer mg/mol on Chapter 3's held element table extended by the chapter's three convergent pins (Ni 58.693, Sn 118.710, Br 79.904) and Ch 34's capsaicin pin (305.418); no float ever enters an operand.
- **The print lane.** Schoolbook long division, digit by digit, half-up decided at the (k+1)-th generated digit; a second print lane (the subtraction lane) cross-proven row by row; a float64 witness typed from independent plain formulas, required to agree at the printed granularity.
- **The gates.** Atom-multiset gates (element-by-element before any mass runs) and distributive-mass lanes on every balance; negative lanes refused by design; 9.0e15 magnitude guards; iterative only — no recursion, no BigInt.
- **The digest.** j31 — imul(31,h)+charCode |0, unpadded hex, over the body text without trailing newline — distinct from Engine 69's FNV-1a and Engines 67/68's djb2/sdbm.
