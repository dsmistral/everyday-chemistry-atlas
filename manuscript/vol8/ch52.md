## Lecture 6 — The Caine Shelf: Ancestors, Esters, and the Amide Line (the recognition read)

*The shelf's last axis is a family named for its ancestor: every member blocks the nerve's sodium channels the same way [convergent], every member wears the -caine suffix, and every member answers one search — the ancestor's numbness without the ancestor's baggage [convergent]. The lecture reads the family the way the chapter reads the whole shelf: the linkage named, the fate traced, the paperwork read, the arrows on the map and never in the hand.*

### 6.1 The ancestor (Vienna, 1884)

Local anesthesia has a birth certificate: Vienna, 1884, the ophthalmologist Carl Koller demonstrating that a cocaine solution applied to the cornea abolishes sensation — the first clinical use of a local anesthetic [convergent]. The ancestor worked, and its baggage — addiction, then control — set the family's whole project: keep the blockade, lose the molecule. The suffix became the family name; the name *lidocaine* itself is xylidine + -caine, the aromatic amine it was built from and the ancestor's name it answers to [convergent].

Cocaine, read only as the map's starting point: C17H21NO4, my = 303.358 [computed: Lab 52.2, Sheet 1; published 303.35 — convergent]. Its molar mass is a cross-chapter literal collision the Canon-Coverage Check names in full at this chapter's end.

### 6.2 The ester shelf: procaine and benzocaine

The first family of answers replaced the ancestor's complicated framework with one seam: an **ester** linking an aromatic end to an amino end. Procaine (Novocaine) is read at recognition level as the PABA ester of diethylaminoethanol [convergent]; benzocaine is PABA's own ethyl ester, ethyl 4-aminobenzoate [convergent]. Both are the two-row ester face of the register's caine story.

The ester seam has one fate and one consequence. The fate: plasma cholinesterase (pseudocholinesterase) hydrolyzes the ester bond, and procaine comes apart to PABA and diethylaminoethanol, both anesthetically inactive [convergent — three independent statements]; Engine 81's B1 verifies the bookkeeping: procaine + water → PABA + DEAE balances on element counts (C13H22N2O3 both sides) and on mass lanes (254330 = 254330) [computed: Lab 52.2, Sheet 2]. Benzocaine's hydrolysis reads the same: benzocaine + water → PABA + ethanol, counts C9H13NO3 both sides, mass 183207 both sides [computed: Sheet 2, B4].

The consequence is the shelf's allergy seam: the esters' shared hydrolysis product, PABA, is the primary sensitizer in ester-local-anesthetic allergy; the amides make no PABA at all, which is why true allergy to one class barely crosses to the other [convergent — the family read stated independently by the allergy literature]. The recognition skill: a reported "caine allergy" is a question about the class, not the family.

The register's benzocaine face is a fence datum in itself: N438, the 2014 benzocaine synthesis video, is a **deleted-episode** — removed by the performer, surviving at description level only, with the 2015 remake (RiTN1EYB-rs) registered-not-gathered [register].
